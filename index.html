Welcome to ATLAS — The Machine Archive

“ATLAS is a global encyclopedia celebrating human engineering and innovation. Explore remarkable machines from around the world, including aircraft, fighter jets, passenger planes, warships, submarines, supercars, trains, construction machinery, spacecraft, satellites, robots and military vehicles.

Discover their origins, understand their engineering, and learn how these machines help shape our world. From historic inventions to modern technological achievements, ATLAS brings the world of machines together in one archive.

Explore the machines. Discover the engineering. Understand the world.”
